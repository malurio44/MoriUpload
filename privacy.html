Privacy policy

Mori uploader is a personal application used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's channels (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to the owner's channel and video information (youtube.readonly) and analytics (yt-analytics.readonly).

What it does with it

Uploads videos the owner created, checks that uploads were processed, and reads aggregate view and retention statistics for those videos. Data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about other YouTube users is collected.

This application uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
